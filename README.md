Have you ever dreamed about being chased by a demon, fighting a demon, or seeing a frightening figure in your sleep?

Demon dreams can feel intense and leave you wondering what they mean. While these dreams may be unsettling, they do not necessarily predict something bad or indicate a supernatural threat.

A demon in a dream may represent fear, stress, guilt, unresolved emotions, or a situation that feels difficult to control. The meaning can depend on the dream's details and your personal experiences.
